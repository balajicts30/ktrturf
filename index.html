01 / About KTRTURF

MORE THAN JUST A TURF.

At KTRTURF, we bring the spirit of the game to life with a premium, multi-sport facility designed for enthusiasts of all ages and skill levels. Whether you're looking to kick off a friendly football match, spike your way through a thrilling volleyball game, or enjoy the fast-paced excitement of box cricket, KTRTURF has it all.

Our state-of-the-art turf is built for performance, comfort, and durability—perfect for casual players, leagues, tournaments, and corporate events. With well-maintained grounds, quality lighting for night play, and a vibrant atmosphere, we ensure an unbeatable sports experience every time you visit.
